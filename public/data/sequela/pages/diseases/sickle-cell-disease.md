# Sickle cell disease

**Gene(s):** `HBB` (β-globin)  
**OMIM:** https://omim.org/entry/603903  
**MONDO:** https://monarchinitiative.org/MONDO:0011382

Sickle cell disease is a hereditary hemoglobinopathy caused by a missense variant in **HBB** (β6 Glu→Val). Deoxygenation promotes hemoglobin polymerization, leading to hemolysis and vaso-occlusive complications. Management includes infection prevention, pain control, hydroxyurea or other disease-modifying therapy, transfusion support, and—in selected cases—curative hematopoietic stem-cell transplantation.
