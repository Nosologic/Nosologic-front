## Summary
Tuberculosis (TB) is caused by *Mycobacterium tuberculosis*. In the context of evolutionary sequela, we track hypotheses where long-standing environmental or population histories (e.g., chronic hypoxia at high altitude and subsequent descent) might shape TB risk profiles in specific populations and places. For evidence, see the evseq page below.
