# Nepal (provenance)

**Type:** Geographical provenance of a cohort showing high incidence of tuberculosis.

## Why this cluster matters
According to Corbett et al. (2022), tuberculosis (TB) incidence among Nepalese immigrants in Australia has been unusually high, particularly within the first years after arrival. This pattern is consistent with hypotheses about high-altitude adaptation, HIF-pathway biology, and descent to normoxia modulating risk profiles.
